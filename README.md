# EV-charging-station-analysis
EV Charging Station Operations Intelligence
Operations don't fail because data is unavailable they fail because critical insights remain hidden. This project transforms EV charging station operational data into actionable intelligence, enabling operators to improve charger utilization, optimize energy distribution, reduce congestion, and make informed business decisions.
Executive Summary
As electric vehicle adoption accelerates, charging infrastructure is becoming a critical operational asset. Every charging session generates valuable operational data, but without proper analysis, opportunities to improve efficiency, customer experience, and revenue are easily overlooked.
In this project, I approached the dataset from an Operations Analyst's perspective rather than simply building a dashboard. My objective was to evaluate how charging stations perform under real operating conditions, identify operational bottlenecks, measure key performance indicators (KPIs), and uncover trends that support strategic and operational decision-making.
Using Power BI and Power Query, I transformed raw charging session data into an interactive operations intelligence dashboard that answers important business questions such as:
•	Which charging stations experience the highest utilization?
•	When does demand peak during the day and week?
•	Which locations generate the highest revenue?
•	How efficiently are charging assets being used?
•	Where can operational improvements increase customer satisfaction and profitability?
Operational Objectives
This project focuses on delivering insights that support operational excellence by:
•	Monitoring charger utilization and performance.
•	Identifying peak demand periods.
•	Evaluating charging session behavior.
•	Measuring energy consumption across stations.
•	Tracking revenue and operational KPIs.
•	Supporting capacity planning and future infrastructure expansion.
Tools Used

Operational Impact
The dashboard provides decision-makers with a clear understanding of station performance, helping them:
•	Improve charger availability during high-demand periods.
•	Optimize maintenance scheduling.
•	Allocate resources more effectively.
•	Increase operational efficiency.
•	Support investment decisions with data.
